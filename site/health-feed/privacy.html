Privacy policy

Jo’s Health Feed is a personal script used only by Jo Hunter, with her own Google account.

What it reads: her own steps, sleep and resting heart rate from the Google Health API, with read-only permissions. It cannot change or delete any health data.

Where the data goes: to Jo’s own computer and her own Google Drive, for her personal tracking, where she and the AI assistant she works with read it. It is not sold or shared with anyone else, and it is not used for advertising.

Other users: none. The app is not offered to anyone else.

Withdrawing access: access can be removed at any time from the Google account’s security settings (third-party connections).
